# Typed persisted intelligence projection

IntelligenceHistory connects the local ledger to the graph and categorical novelty evaluators. It serializes typed signals, links and approved category windows; restores typed records; verifies record receipt and availability agreement; binds feature partition to canonical entity identity; queries the knowledge-time cutoff for graph evidence and the earlier approval-time cutoff for training. It returns snapshot receipts alongside the evaluation result. A future relationship cannot affect the earlier evaluation. Exceeding ledger limits yields UNKNOWN rather than a partial match. External completeness is still asserted by the caller. Graph signal and relationship queries are separate snapshots; concurrent appends and backdating can affect reproducibility. This is an offline lab adapter, not an authenticated or transactionally consistent native graph service. Graph signals are not yet a production risk score ledger. No finding publication or scheduler registration is provided.
